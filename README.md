# Acronym Annunciator ✈️

A study game for the FAA Instrument Rating oral exam. The acronyms, mnemonics and quick facts behind the IR checkride, set up like an annunciator panel. You light every panel by getting them right.

**▶ Play:** **https://sofocles.github.io/ir-acronym-annunciator/**. It works on a phone, and there's nothing to install.

## Modes

- **Spell:** one letter at a time. Pick what it stands for. The wrong answers start with the same letter.
- **Order:** every item, scrambled. Tap them in acronym order.
- **Lightning:** beat the clock. Streaks buy time and mistakes cost it.
- **Facts:** numbers, definitions and descent math, with no clock.

Progress is saved in your browser on your device.

## Leaderboard

The live leaderboard runs on the Claude version of the game. In this copy you can pick a call sign, tap **Copy my score card**, and send the card to Sebastian, who adds it to the board.

## Note

This is a study aid, not a source of truth. Check everything against the current 14 CFR, AIM, Instrument Flying Handbook and PHAK, and your CFII.
